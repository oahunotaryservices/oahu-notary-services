Privacy

Website privacy information.

This website is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Contact forms

The appointment form does not submit information to a website database. When you choose to send the form, your device opens your email application with the details filled in. Your email provider then handles that message according to its own privacy practices.

Documents

Use the Secure Client Upload button for sensitive documents. Do not place confidential information in ordinary website code or public comments.

Third-party links

This website may link to review platforms, email, phone/text functions, document-upload services and government websites. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Analytics

No advertising tracker is included by default. If analytics or a review widget is added later, this notice should be updated to describe it.

Contact

Questions about website privacy can be sent to OahuNotaryServices@gmail.com.
