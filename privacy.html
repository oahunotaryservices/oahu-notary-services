Privacy

Website privacy information.

This GitHub Pages version is intentionally designed to minimize collection of personal information.

Contact forms

The appointment form on this website does not submit information to a database. When you choose to send the form, your device opens your email application with the details filled in. Your email provider then handles that message according to its own privacy practices.

Documents

Do not upload documents to GitHub or place confidential information in public website code. The Secure Client Upload button sends you to the separate client-upload service used by Oahu Notary Services.

Third-party links

This website may link to review platforms, email, phone/text functions, document-upload services and government websites. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Analytics

No analytics or advertising tracker is included in this website package by default. If analytics or a review widget is added later, this notice should be updated to describe it.

Contact

Questions about website privacy can be sent to OahuNotaryServices@gmail.com.
