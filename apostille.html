How It Works

The correct route depends on who issued the document.

A notarized private document, a Hawaiʻi vital record, a court document and a federal document can follow different paths. We first identify the document type and destination country, then quote the facilitation work.

Important distinction

Oahu Notary Services does not issue apostilles. Apostilles and authentications are issued by the appropriate government authority. We act as a notary and/or facilitator/courier for the steps you authorize.

Government processing times and acceptance decisions are outside our control.

Common Questions

Documents do not all follow the same apostille process.

Can you apostille my document yourself?

No. We can facilitate the process, but the apostille itself is issued by the appropriate government authority.

What about a notarized Hawaiʻi document?

Hawaiʻi's current process requires an additional certification step for the notary before a notarized document is submitted to the Lieutenant Governor. We can help coordinate that route.

What about an FBI background check?

That is a federal document and does not use the Hawaiʻi state apostille route. Federal documents generally need the appropriate federal authentication process.

Can you guarantee a deadline?

No. We can estimate our handling time, but government processing times are outside our control.
