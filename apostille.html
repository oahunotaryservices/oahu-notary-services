Apostille Assistance

Help getting Hawaiʻi documents through the right authentication route.

Apostille and authentication requests depend on the document, issuing authority and destination country. We can help with the notary and coordination steps that apply to your document.

How It Works

A coordinated process from document review to return.

1

Review

Identify the document type, destination country and whether notarization or another certification step is needed.

2

Notarization

Complete the required notarization when the document is eligible and the signer can meet Hawaiʻi notary requirements.

3

Processing

Coordinate authorized submission to the appropriate government office for apostille or authentication processing.

4

Return

Arrange pickup, local delivery or shipping back to you after processing.

Bring / Send

What we need to quote your request.

  • Destination country
  • Document type and issuing authority
  • Number of documents
  • Whether the documents are originals, certified copies or notarized documents
  • Your requested completion or return method
  • Any deadline you are working with

Important distinction

Oahu Notary Services does not issue apostilles. Apostilles and authentications are issued by the appropriate government authority. We act as a notary and/or facilitator or courier for the steps you authorize.

Government processing times and acceptance decisions are outside our control.

Common Questions

Documents do not all follow the same apostille process.

Can you apostille my document yourself?

No. We can facilitate the process, but the apostille itself is issued by the appropriate government authority.

What about a notarized Hawaiʻi document?

Hawaiʻi's process includes an additional certification step for the notary before a notarized document is submitted for apostille processing. We can help coordinate the route that applies.

What about an FBI background check?

That is a federal document and does not use the Hawaiʻi state apostille route. Federal documents generally require the appropriate federal authentication process.

Can you guarantee a deadline?

No. We can estimate our handling time, but government processing times are outside our control.
