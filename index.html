Before the Appointment

A few things make the signing go much smoother.

  • Have an acceptable, current government-issued photo ID available.
  • Bring the complete document and any required attachments.
  • Do not sign ahead of time when the signature must be witnessed by the notary.
  • Confirm any witness requirements with the document preparer or receiving agency.
  • If you are unsure which notarial act is required, ask the document preparer or receiving agency before the appointment.
