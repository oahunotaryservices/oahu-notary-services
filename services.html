Our Services

Mobile notarizations for everyday documents and complex appointments.

We travel throughout Oʻahu and can meet at homes, offices, hospitals, care facilities, hotels, correctional facilities and other convenient locations.

Acknowledgments

For documents requiring the signer to acknowledge a signature before a commissioned notary.

Jurats & Sworn Statements

For documents requiring an oath or affirmation and a signature completed in the notary's presence.

Affidavits

Mobile notarization for affidavits and other sworn written statements.

Powers of Attorney

Mobile appointments for powers of attorney, including hospital and care-facility signings.

Estate Planning Signings

Trusts, wills where notarization is requested, advance health care directives, powers of attorney and related attorney-prepared signing packages.

Real Estate & Loan Signings

Deeds, seller packages, buyer packages, refinances, HELOCs and other real-estate or loan signing assignments.

Vehicle Documents

Bills of sale, title or transfer-related forms, affidavits and authorization documents.

Family & Consent Documents

Travel consent, adoption-related documents, divorce-related documents, prenuptial agreements and authorizations.

Court & Legal Documents

Settlement agreements, answers to interrogatories, affidavits and other documents prepared for legal proceedings.

Hospital / Care Facility

Mobile service for hospitals, nursing homes, rehabilitation facilities and care homes, including longer or mobility-sensitive appointments.

Correctional Facility

Appointments requiring advance institution approval, visitor clearance or coordination with facility staff.

Certified Copies

Copy certification where permitted. Government-issued vital records such as birth, death and marriage certificates are excluded.

Additional Help

More than just the stamp.

  • Document printing by prior arrangement
  • Scan-back services when requested
  • Mailing and shipping coordination
  • In-person delivery on Oʻahu
  • Apostille/authentication facilitation
  • Secure pre-appointment document upload

Not sure whether we handle your document?

Send the document name, where it will be used, the number of signers and the appointment location. We can confirm whether it is within our services and what information we need to quote the appointment.
