Policies

Booking and cancellation.

  • Payment is due at the time of service.
  • All fees are agreed upon before the appointment is confirmed.
  • Fees are non-refundable once the reserved service is performed.
  • Cancel or reschedule with at least 3 hours' notice to avoid a scheduling fee.
  • Less than 3 hours' notice or a no-show may result in a $30 scheduling fee.
  • Emergency exceptions may be considered based on the circumstances.
