Standard Mobile Notary

Travel / meeting / appointment-time fee by service area.

The area price is the starting fee for travel, meeting time and the appointment. Town / South Oʻahu has the lowest starting mobile rate; exact travel pricing is confirmed from the address. The Hawaiʻi statutory notarial fee is listed separately.

Town / Honolulu starting example: $70 before GET

$65 starting travel / meeting fee + one $5 notarized signature. Exact travel pricing is confirmed from the appointment address.

Notarization / Signature: $5 each

For acknowledgments, Hawaiʻi law sets the fee at $5 for each party signing. For an oath/jurat, the fee is $5 for the administration of the oath.

Hawaiʻi GET: 4.712%

Cancellation / Rescheduling

At least 3 hours' notice: no scheduling fee. Less than 3 hours' notice or a no-show may be subject to a $30 scheduling fee. Emergency exceptions may be considered.

Refunds

Payments are final and non-refundable, except where a refund is required by law. Our Acceptance Guarantee remains available for an error in our notarial portion.
