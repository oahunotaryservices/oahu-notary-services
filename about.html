About Oahu Notary Services

A local mobile notary built around accuracy, convenience and accountability.

Oahu Notary Services is locally owned and operated on Oʻahu, serving individuals, families, attorneys, businesses, care facilities and signing professionals by appointment.

Meet Stacey N. Spirz

Your Oʻahu mobile notary.

Stacey has worked with legal documents as a legal assistant since 2014 and has served as a commissioned Hawaiʻi Notary Public since 2023. Oahu Notary Services was created to make professional notarization more accessible by bringing the appointment to the client.

Appointments can be arranged at homes, offices, hospitals, care facilities, correctional facilities and other convenient locations throughout Oʻahu.
