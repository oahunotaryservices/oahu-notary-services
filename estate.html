Before the appointment

Have the complete attorney-prepared package available and confirm whether witnesses are required. If you want us to review the signing logistics first, send the package through the secure client upload.

Important

The notary handles the notarization—not the legal decisions.

Oahu Notary Services does not explain the legal effect of estate-planning documents, select provisions, or provide legal advice. Questions about document meaning, choices or legal consequences should be directed to the attorney or document preparer.

Helpful appointment information: When contacting us, include the signing location, preferred date/time, number of signers, whether witnesses are required, and the approximate size of the estate-planning package.
