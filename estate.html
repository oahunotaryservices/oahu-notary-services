What This Service Is For

More room for a larger signing package.

  • Attorney-prepared trusts and estate-planning packages
  • Powers of attorney and advance health care directives
  • Deeds and related documents included with an estate plan
  • Multiple signature and notarization locations
  • Witness and identification logistics
  • Home, office, hospital and care-facility appointments when arranged

Before the appointment

Have the complete attorney-prepared package available and confirm whether witnesses are required. Providing your own qualified witness(es) is preferred. If you need help, ask about witness availability when scheduling; one witness may be available by advance arrangement for +$25 , subject to availability and the document's requirements.

Important

The notary handles the notarization—not the legal decisions.

Oahu Notary Services does not explain the legal effect of estate-planning documents, select provisions, or provide legal advice. Questions about document meaning, choices or legal consequences should be directed to the attorney or document preparer.

Helpful appointment information: When contacting us, include the signing location, preferred date/time, number of signers, whether witnesses are required, whether you will provide your own witness(es) or need to ask about availability for one witness, and the approximate size of the estate-planning package.
